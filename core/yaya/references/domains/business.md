# business — Business communication

Scope: work emails, status updates, proposals, product explanations and customer
notices. Preparing text never sends it, approves a plan or binds an organization.

## Editing decisions

- Make the requested action or supported update easy to find. Keep owner, due date,
  recipient, dependencies and approval state when supplied. Do not invent them.
- Distinguish a proposal, estimate, target, commitment and completed delivery. A
  confident tone cannot upgrade “计划”“拟”“待审批” into a promise or accomplishment.
- Keep price basis, currency, tax/fee treatment, service scope, exclusions and
  availability limits attached to the offer. Preserve material tradeoffs and risk.
- Explain a concrete benefit with supported facts. Remove unsupported promotional
  padding without replacing it with invented adoption, performance or customer data.
- Match the reader and situation: a customer outage notice may need calm directness;
  a routine internal request may be brief. Do not force jokes, excitement or a fixed
  brand personality into all business text.

Original example (edit): “为了进一步推进高效协同，我们希望您能够在周五前确认报价；
交付时间需在采购审批后确定。” → “请在周五前确认报价；交付时间需在采购审批后确定。”

Preserve: “该功能目前仅向试用账户开放，正式上线时间尚未确定。” Do not present
the feature as generally available or set an invented launch date.

Rationale: [Mailchimp's voice and tone guide](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/)
separates a consistent voice from context-dependent tone and prioritizes clarity.
Yaya uses that distinction without importing Mailchimp's brand identity or humor.
